================================================================================
ECLIPSE: Echoes of the Forgotten — Game Logic & Developer Onboarding Guide
================================================================================

Welcome to the ECLIPSE development team! This document is a complete technical
guide to the game logic, system architecture, physics formulas, combat pipelines,
and state machines powering the project.

Whether you are implementing a new enemy, designing a room, adding an ability,
or tuning gameplay feel, this guide explains how the entire codebase connects
and how to contribute without breaking existing functionality.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PROJECT OVERVIEW & DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Title: ECLIPSE: Echoes of the Forgotten
- Genre: 2D Action Metroidvania / Gothic Platformer
- Engine: Godot Engine 4.7.2 (GDScript, Forward+ / D3D12)
- Primary Influences: Hollow Knight, Castlevania: Symphony of the Night, Silksong
- Protagonist: The Aristocratic Occult Detective
  * Wields an engraved silver Cane Sword (rapier) with electric cyan/lunar light trails.
  * Channels occult aether into glowing Ethereal Tarot Cards for Focus Healing.
  * Clad in a tall obsidian silk top hat (royal purple band, brass buckle),
    tailored waistcoat, pocket watch chain, and coachman's capelet with purple silk lining.

THE 4 CORE DESIGN PILLARS:
1. Immediate, Tight Locomotion:
   No sluggish floatiness. Snappy acceleration (2000 px/s^2), instant stopping
   friction (2400 px/s^2), variable jump height, coyote time, and pre-landing jump buffering.
2. Readable, Fair Combat:
   Every enemy and boss attack has distinct startup telegraphs, active damage
   frames, and post-attack recovery openings. The player has a 0.05s startup,
   single-hit tracking, directional attacks (up, forward, down-pogo), and hit-stop freeze frames.
3. Risk & Rhythm Resource Loop:
   Striking enemies harvests Soul/Aether (+11 per hit). Stationary Focus Healing
   channels 33 Soul over 0.85s to heal 1 mask, but leaves the player completely vulnerable.
4. Interconnected World & Ability Gating:
   Hand-crafted rooms connected via two-way transition gates with camera viewport
   clamping, checkpoint resting benches, and persistent save data.

3. THE 2D COLLISION LAYER MATRIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

All physics interactions in ECLIPSE rely on this strict 9-layer allocation:

Layer 1 (Bit 1):   World
  * Description: Solid geometry, floors, walls, ceilings, one-way platforms, closed arena gates.
  * Masks: Solid bodies.

Layer 2 (Bit 2):   Player
  * Description: Player CharacterBody2D physics body.
  * Masks: Layer 1 (World).

Layer 3 (Bit 4):   Enemies
  * Description: Enemy CharacterBody2D physics bodies.
  * Masks: Layer 1 (World).

Layer 4 (Bit 8):   PlayerHitbox
  * Description: Player Cane Sword attack volume (Hitbox).
  * Masks: Layer 7 (EnemyHurtbox), Layer 8 (Hazards).

Layer 5 (Bit 16):  PlayerHurtbox
  * Description: Player vulnerable damage receiver (Hurtbox).
  * Masks: Layer 6 (EnemyHitbox), Layer 8 (Hazards).

Layer 6 (Bit 32):  EnemyHitbox
  * Description: Enemy attacks & contact damage (Hitbox).
  * Masks: Layer 5 (PlayerHurtbox).

Layer 7 (Bit 64):  EnemyHurtbox
  * Description: Enemy vulnerable damage receiver (Hurtbox).
  * Masks: Layer 4 (PlayerHitbox).

Layer 8 (Bit 128): Hazards
  * Description: Spikes, acid, floor shockwaves.
  * Masks: Deals damage to Layer 5; rebounds Layer 4 pogo.

Layer 9 (Bit 256): Interactables
  * Description: Checkpoint benches, ability altars, NPC dialogue triggers.
  * Masks: Layer 2 (Player).

4. PLAYER MECHANICS & LOCOMOTION LOGIC
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The controller lives in `scripts/player/player_controller.gd`.
All tuning constants are stored in `scripts/player/player_config.gd`
(`res://resources/player/default_player_config.tres`).

LOCOMOTION STATE MACHINE (PlayerController.State):
1. IDLE: Grounded, zero horizontal input.
2. RUN: Grounded, running with horizontal velocity (max_speed = 260.0 px/s).
3. JUMP: Airborne, moving upward (velocity.y < 0).
4. FALL: Airborne, falling downward (velocity.y >= 0).
5. LAND: Ground touchdown recovery (land_duration = 0.08s), cancelable into RUN or JUMP.
6. WALL_SLIDE: Clinging against a vertical wall, sliding downward (speed clamped to 120.0 px/s).
7. WALL_JUMP: Airborne leap away from wall (velocity = Vector2(300, -480)).
8. DASH: High-speed horizontal burst (dash_speed = 550.0 px/s, duration = 0.16s, zero gravity).
9. FOCUS: Grounded stationary healing channel (velocity locked, tarot cards circling).
10. MANTLE: Clinging to a platform ledge corner (velocity zeroed, awaiting vault or drop).

KINEMATICS & FORGIVENESS FORMULAS:
* Gravity Calculation:
    jump_gravity = (2.0 * jump_height) / (time_to_peak * time_to_peak)  # ~1660 px/s^2
    jump_velocity = -jump_gravity * time_to_peak                         # -564.7 px/s
    fall_gravity = jump_gravity * fall_gravity_multiplier                 # ~2241 px/s^2 (1.35x)
* Variable Jump Cutoff:
    Releasing Jump early multiplies upward velocity by 0.5x, allowing short taps (24-32px)
    or full holds (96px).
* Coyote Time (0.12s):
    Leaving a ledge gives 7-8 frames of grace where jump is still valid.
* Jump Buffering (0.12s):
    Tapping Jump slightly before landing queues the jump and executes on touchdown.
* Air Dash:
    Exactly 1 air dash per airtime. Refreshed by touching ground, sliding on a wall,
    wall jumping, or landing a downward pogo rebound.
* Double Jump (Wings of the Lunar Eclipse):
    Gated by `double_jump_unlocked` (and PlayerData.has_ability("double_jump")).
    Mid-air Jump applies double_jump_velocity (-460.0 px/s). Refreshes on ground,
    wall slide, wall jump, or pogo bounce.
* Athletic Mantle / Ledge Grab:
    3-ray direct space query detects solid chest wall (y - 18px), open head air clearance (y - 42px),
    and solid ledge lip surface. Pressing JUMP or UP vaults onto the surface. Pressing DOWN drops.

5. COMBAT & RESOURCE LOOP LOGIC
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CANE SWORD ATTACK LIFECYCLE:
  [NONE] -> [STARTUP: 0.05s] -> [ACTIVE: 0.07s] -> [RECOVERY: 0.10s] -> [NONE]
* Startup (0.05s): Wind-up anticipation. Facing direction locks.
* Active (0.07s): Hitbox enabled on Layer 4 (PlayerHitbox).
  - Forward Slash: (facing * 26, -20), size 36x30 px.
  - Upward Slash (holding UP): (0, -48), size 40x32 px.
  - Downward Pogo (airborne + holding DOWN): (0, 4), size 36x24 px.
* Recovery (0.10s): Hitbox disabled. Input buffering queues next swing.
* Single-Hit Tracking: `_hit_targets` tracks struck entities to guarantee exactly
  1 hit per swing per entity.
* Hit-Stop Freeze: Landed strikes freeze attacker and target for 0.06s.
* Camera Shake: Quadratic trauma-decay camera shake (trauma^2).

AERIAL DOWNWARD POGO REBOUND:
* Striking an enemy or hazard with a downward slash rebounds player upward with -460.0 px/s,
  resets coyote time, and refreshes air dash and double jump charges.

SOUL (AETHER) & FOCUS HEALING LOOP:
* Soul Harvesting: Hitting an enemy grants +11 Soul (max cap 99 = 3 charges of 33).
  Hazard spikes do NOT award soul.
* Focus Healing: Grounded, stationary, holding Focus for 0.85s consumes 33 Soul
  to restore 1 Health Mask. Any movement or damage immediately cancels channel.
* Visuals: Luminous Ethereal Tarot Cards orbit the detective with cyan celestial runes
  and a spinning arcane runic circle on the floor.

6. ENEMY AI & BOSS ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

All hostile entities inherit from `EnemyBase` (scripts/enemies/enemy_base.gd).
State lifecycle: IDLE -> PATROL -> ALERT -> ATTACK -> HURT -> DEAD.
Includes knockback resistance, white damage flash, and currency drops on defeat.

MOBILE ENEMY ARCHETYPES:
1. Walker (Scavenger Husk): Ground patrol (50 px/s), ledge/wall turnaround, 0.35s alert,
   and 180 px/s forward melee lunge.
2. Flyer (Gloom Bat): Sinusoidal hover bob at zero gravity, downward player detection,
   220 px/s dive swoop, and parabolic climb return to roost.
3. Charger (Armored Beetle): Heavy 6 HP tank, 50% knockback resistance, 0.45s roar telegraph,
   280 px/s forward charge, and 1.2s wall-stun upon hitting solid barriers.

FIRST BOSS: ARCHIVE WARDEN (ArchiveWarden):
* Located in `ancient_chamber.tscn`. 30 HP, 85% knockback resistance, active hyper-armor (poise).
* Phase 1 (HP > 15):
  - Overhead Mace Slam: 0.50s windup -> ground impact -> floor GroundShockwave -> 0.85s recovery.
  - Forward Thrust Charge: 0.40s windup -> 340 px/s charge (heavy knockback) -> 0.85s recovery.
* Phase 2 Enrage (HP <= 15):
  - 1.20s invulnerable roar with screen shake; activates permanent celestial purple aura.
  - Accelerated windups (0.40s) and recovery windows (0.45s).
  - Leaping Ground Pound: Leaps high (-520 px/s) toward player -> impact -> dual opposing
    floor shockwaves traveling left and right.
* Arena Gates & Persistence: Western and Eastern portcullises seal on encounter trigger,
  permanently unlock on defeat. Defeat persists in PlayerData (`boss_warden_defeated = true`).

7. WORLD SCENE STREAMING & PERSISTENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ROOM STREAMING PIPELINE (GameManager):
1. Player enters TransitionPoint gateway.
2. GameManager locks player inputs and initiates 0.20s fade to black.
3. Current room node is freed; target room is instantiated.
4. Player is positioned at paired `<gate_id>_Spawn` Marker2D with safe entry offset.
5. Camera limits snap to RoomBase boundaries.
6. Screen fades in (0.20s) and player input is unlocked.

GOTHIC BENCH CHECKPOINTS (BenchCheckpoint):
* Rest restores 5/5 health masks and 99/99 soul.
* Saves game data to disk (`user://save_slot_1.res` or `.json`).
* Emits `bench_rested`, prompting RoomBase to cleanly respawn defeated room enemies.

PLAYERDATA LEDGER (scripts/core/player_data.gd):
* Flat serializable ledger: health, soul, currency, mask shards, unlocked abilities,
  visited rooms, story flags, and spawn anchors.
* Collecting 4 Mask Shards increments max_health by +1 and fully heals the player.

VERTICAL SLICE ROOMS:
1. Forgotten Entrance (forgotten_entrance.tscn): Sanctuary bench, arches, gate to Archive.
2. Sunken Archive (sunken_archive.tscn): Archival halls, vertical drop, enemies, gate to Chamber.
3. Ancient Chamber (ancient_chamber.tscn): Boss arena, 1000px runway, elevated platforms, gates.
4. Celestial Sanctuary (celestial_sanctuary.tscn): Oasis with bench, Wings altar, and an 180px
   elevated balcony holding a secret Mask Shard requiring Double Jump to scale.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. GOLDEN RULES FOR CONTRIBUTORS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Never Hardcode Numbers:
   Place speeds, gravities, timers, and damage in `PlayerConfig` or exported variables.
2. Explicit Static Typing:
   Always write `var speed: float = 260.0` and `func do_action() -> void:`.
3. Decouple via Signals:
   Call down directly to child nodes; emit signals upward to parent/manager nodes.
4. Preserve Single-Hit per Swing:
   Track struck nodes in an array/dictionary to prevent multi-hit-per-frame bugs.
5. Respect Collision Layers:
   Strictly follow the 9 layers in Section 3.
6. Keep All 834 Tests Green:
   Never introduce changes that break existing physics or world tests.
7. Zero Git Initialization Unless Instructed:
   Do not initialize git or alter version control files without explicit authorization.
